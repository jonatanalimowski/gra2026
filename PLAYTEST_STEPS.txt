PLAYTEST 2026-10-31 - SUMMARY AND LIST OF STEPS
(4 testers: H, D, M, B)

SUMMARY
-------
What works: the game is enjoyable (M), sounds aren't distracting (M), the starting weapons
are similarly strong (M), level design with obstacles adds a lot (H), B liked the "sniper".

Main problem: upgrades. D, M and B independently say they're too rare or don't change
the playstyle. This is the most common complaint, and it concerns what ROADMAP already
calls the thinnest part of the game (the pact pool). The boss gives only +10% stat upgrades.

Combat readability and fairness: enemies appear immediately on entering a room, telegraphs
are too short and hard to see, and taking damage has no strong signal.

Other: not much happens at the start (H), no reason to dash (D), too little healing (B),
no way to back out of the weapon choice (M, B).

Conclusion: do a short post-playtest patch before meta progression (v0.13). Meta won't
fix these complaints, and steps 1-5 are cheap.


LIST OF STEPS
-------------

5. Retest healing (B)
   Not important for now

B. UPGRADES (the most important playtest complaint)

6. More pacts that change the playstyle (D, M, B)
   Go from 9 to about 15, mostly behavior effects and OnEnemyKilled / OnRoomCleared hooks
   (only Explode on Kill uses them so far). Curses should change play too, not just -X%.

7. More frequent choices (D, M, H)
   First altar early on the route (also helps with "not much happens at the start").
   The altar sits at the end of a side branch, so check whether testers found it at all.
   Then try 2 altars per floor.

8. A boss reward that changes something (M)
   Design decision: add one behavior-changing option alongside the stats.
   This loosens pillar 1 in ROADMAP (curse-free means stats only), so do 6-7 first and
   only then judge whether it's needed. If it is, update the pillar.

C. NEW CONTENT

9. A reason to dash (D)
   An enemy with an attack you can't walk away from: an expanding shockwave (new scene
   modeled on Explosion), crossed on the dash's i-frames.
   Give the same wave to the Slime King when landing from a super jump.
   Also: MakeInvulnerable doesn't handle overlapping timers (an earlier timer ends a later
   invulnerability too soon). Fix it if it gets a second caller.

10. Level design with obstacles (H)
    Add obstacles to the existing combat rooms. Improves every fight with no new code.

11. Building with intent (D)
    Cheap version before the Forge: pick the primary and heavy spell separately before a run
    (Loadout builds a copy of WandData, without modifying the .tres). Combinations need balancing.
    The full version is the Loadout already planned for v0.14.

D. AFTERWARDS

12. Another playtest with the same questions, then meta progression (v0.13) per ROADMAP.
